This edition adopts the Personal HR OS product name and selects the coordinator and employee lifecycle procedure. The original synthetic event and roster files, fictional matching rules and lifecycle handover template are retained. The public source contains no completed employee records.

The installer copies the listed workspace files and verifies the saved copies with SHA-256. It adds two discovery links under the new workspace’s `.agents/skills/`. The installed workspace retains its own records and local data.
