# Employee lifecycle record preparation

**Draft · SSC operations, with HRIS support.** Confirm the event, authority, near-term endpoint, applicable rule version and responsible owner. Open the [record codebook](../../../Work/Record%20codebook.md), [Case Index](../../../Work/Case%20Index.md), [Work Record](../../../Work/Templates/Work%20Record.md), [shared operating methods](../../../Context/Shared%20operating%20methods.md) and [lifecycle handover](../../../Work/Templates/Lifecycle%20Record%20Handover.md).

1. **Intake and identity.** Register or resume one case under its original pillar. Read the event source, dated roster or approved identity map, document checklist and supplied approval evidence. State the primary event and person keys, normalisation, fallback or none, duplicate policy and unmatched treatment before matching. Hold ambiguous duplicate event IDs, duplicate roster identities, unmatched people and contradictory records. Preserve each raw row and report every visible reason. An apparent spelling or name match cannot resolve an identity.
2. **Prepare per event.** For joining, compare the approved recruitment handover or other owner-specified source, proposed record fields, start date and required document references against the receiving source. For movement, compare the effective date and existing values with the dated roster before preparing the approved changes. For offboarding, verify the authorised end date and scope, then list the requested status and account or access fulfilment dependencies. Confirm the case-specific checklist, availability and authorised verifier. Availability alone does not establish authenticity. Hold missing, expired or conflicting evidence as directed by the applicable rule.
3. **Separate stages.** Show source comparison and proposed fields, decision evidence and HR confirmation separately. Partial draft preparation is possible when authorised. A clean record can be ready for HR review with no decision or confirmation if that is the agreed endpoint. An outbound system, access or payroll request requires the separately applicable evidence and effective date. A later payroll handover requires a separately authorised pay instruction and receiving owner. Do not infer pay terms from a joining or movement decision.
4. **Handover and readback.** The named HR reviewer accepts or holds the exact prepared version. For a separately authorised system or account handoff, identify the minimum approved fields, effective time, destination owner, purpose and permitted action. Record request, receipt, owner-confirmed update or fulfilment, and independent verification as separate checkpoints supported by the corresponding evidence. Payroll receives only approved changes for its own eligibility review. Return exceptions to their source owner.
5. **Repeat and correct.** Reopen the saved handover and case, verify coverage against source IDs and record the next owner for each hold. Apply only a valid owner-supplied correction in the same case. Retain prior material findings and recheck invalidated HR, payroll or access checkpoints. A previously accepted version cannot certify changed fields.

The [fictional lifecycle pilot](../../../Context/Guides/Lifecycle%20Pilot.md) ends with a saved draft handover. The designated human owner reviews the evidence and decides whether any future use is authorised.
