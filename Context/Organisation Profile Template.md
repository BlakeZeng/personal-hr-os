# Organisation Profile Template

Complete a copy in the separately controlled private deployment using the destination organisation's current authorised sources. This template contains no organisation facts. Unknowns remain Not confirmed and hold only the affected decision/action; they do not prevent a bounded planning draft.

| Area | Required local information | Value | Source owner and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Organisation | Relevant entities, business context and jurisdictions | Not confirmed | Not confirmed |
| Direction | Human owner, task requester and decision authority | Not confirmed | Not confirmed |
| Governance | Available governance specialist or owner who accepts draft/readiness decisions | Not confirmed | Not confirmed |
| Sources | Authoritative systems/documents, versions, owners and permitted scope | Not confirmed | Not confirmed |
| Data | Identity keys, fields, populations, matching and exception treatment | Not confirmed | Not confirmed |
| Rules | Current policy/rule sources, effective dates and conflict owner | Not confirmed | Not confirmed |
| Actions | Precise read, draft, write, communication and submission permissions | Not confirmed | Not confirmed |
| Work records | Private store, local timezone and case namespace/allocation owner | Not confirmed | Not confirmed |
| Capabilities | Verified tools, skill availability and limitations | Not confirmed | Not confirmed |
| Handover | Receiving owners, acceptance checks and completion endpoints | Not confirmed | Not confirmed |
| Maintenance | Source-change triggers, review owner and versioning | Not confirmed | Not confirmed |

Record approval, applicability and uncertainty with each local value. An account reference is not a credential; retain secrets in the destination's approved secret store. This profile grants no standing action permission.
