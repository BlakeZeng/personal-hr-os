# Shared operating methods

Use these controls with the owning function's instructions. Record the agreed checks in the existing work record.

| Work | Action and verification |
|---|---|
| Sources and spreadsheets | Identify the authoritative owner, revision, cutoff, population and grain. Retain original values and row references. Confirm the primary key and allowed normalization. Use a fallback only with explicit rules and uniqueness checks. Hold duplicate, empty or ambiguous keys and reconcile unmatched rows from both sides. Reopen the saved result and reconcile inputs, included items, held items and totals. |
| Documents | Check the source identifier, version, effective date, approval and audience. Record missing attachments and discrepancies. Store references in the permitted location. Use the minimum fields needed for the receiver's work. |
| Handover and communication | Name the recipient role, permitted channel, exact subject and expected outcome. Prepare the record with its checked source version, remaining holds and next action. Confirm sending authority before transmitting. Capture receiving evidence or keep receipt open. |
| Receiving work | Open the exact version, reconcile its scope and identify any held rows. A recipient can accept the verified subset or hold the output with a reason and named returning owner. Record what was actually received, approved or performed. |
| Learning and reports | State the question, what was checked, the supported finding, its scope and next owner. Move reusable findings to the method owner. Test an authorised change on the affected inputs before treating it as implemented. |

Use the [lifecycle handover form](../Work/Templates/Lifecycle%20Record%20Handover.md) and [case codebook](../Work/Record%20codebook.md). All numeric thresholds, matching rules and authorised recipients for live work come from the adopter's current sources.
